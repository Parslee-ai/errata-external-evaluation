# Prospective custody and sealed-bundle requirements

Status: **no outside custodian or encrypted intake endpoint is admitted yet**.
Do not send hidden material to the repository owner, a public issue, or a pull
request.

## Custodian admission

Before receiving bytes, an outside custodian publishes a signed statement that
binds their identity, public encryption and signing keys, endpoint, allowed
payload types, acknowledgement and rejection receipts, clock source, retention
and deletion schedule, incident response, shutdown procedure, and the rule that
candidate developers receive only candidate-visible projections before
closure. The author and outcome adjudicator countersign role separation and
conflict declarations. A public eligibility receipt precedes transfer.

## Real-software sealed bundle

The custodian commits, without revealing hidden content, to:

- source origin, license, submission authority, fixed revision, and clean-tree
  digest;
- pre-existing outcome mandate and its timestamp;
- native outcome evaluator and independently checkable non-win truth oracle;
- ordinary-access and explicit-authority charter, network/dependency policy,
  shutdown, rollback, and escalation;
- unannounced perturbation and realistic misleading signal;
- incumbent workflow, assistance, cost, and evidence;
- raw and briefed candidate-visible mandates, with no winning-action leakage;
- isolated arm workspaces and blinded case identifier; and
- author, custodian, and adjudicator signatures plus the receipt chain.

The public ledger freezes case digests and arm order before any arm receives
case bytes. A case rejected before exposure retains a rejection receipt; a case
cannot be replaced after the first arm begins.

## Synthetic sealed bundle

The custodian holds authored source/runtime, renderer twins, evaluator, oracle,
non-enumeration proof and verifier, draw entropy, arm inputs, corruption
intervention, and analysis inputs. The candidate developers receive none of
those bytes before closure. The custodian runs the committed proof verifier and
OS confinement, issues hash-chained execution receipts, and releases only the
prospectively declared public record.

Any breach, role overlap, answer leakage, post-draw protocol change, or
unreceipted material access invalidates the affected case or capsule.
