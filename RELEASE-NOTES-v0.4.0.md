postdate the frozen model cutoff. All eight have green ordinary focused tests
at the broken base, a withheld upstream regression that fails there, and both
regression and baseline passing with the accepted implementation. The final
four are selected without replacement by the first predeclared public beacon
strictly after this immutable release. There is no redraw.

The raw candidate is the same `gpt-5.6-sol` general-purpose direct policy on
every case. The 32-row ledger also retains requirements-briefed, duplicate

corrupted, upstream-oracle, and upstream-incumbent rows. Agent outputs are
scored only by evaluator-held regression behavior plus two clean baseline
reruns, authority, budget, and replay evidence. Correct non-wins are admitted
only for evidence-backed missing local execution authority with a concrete
minimum recovery condition.

case choice, but this release does not establish independent cohort design,
population representativeness, synthetic replication, or any model result. At
publication: zero draw, zero reservations, and zero scored attempts.
