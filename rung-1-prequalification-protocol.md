# Rung-1 real-software prequalification

Status: **prospective root-free protocol; no case has been selected or
inspected**

This is the first cheap, honest prequalification of `errata pursue` on real
unfamiliar software work. It is not the broader externally admissible Lane-A
evaluation, a benchmark, a paper, or North-Star admission. The narrower
question is whether the frozen pursuit machinery earns its cost against a
strong direct Codex agent on the same bounded substrate.

The content-addressed machine-readable freeze is written by
`scripts/freeze_rung1_prequalification.py`. That manifest is authoritative
where this prose and the manifest differ.

## Freeze boundary

The candidate is the `errata pursue` implementation at the exact source commit
and file digests in the manifest. Its model, prompt, tools, authority policy,
budgets, stopping rules, scoring, direct baseline, and attempt accounting are
frozen before any candidate repository is selected or inspected. The freeze
contains no case identity, repository root, revision, mandate, suspected
defect, or case-derived fact.

A mechanism change after selection starts a new cohort. It cannot be scored on
any case selected for this cohort. Evaluation-only recording and independent
auditing may continue, but may not alter either arm's information or action
surface.

## Candidate admission

Candidates are considered in a retained ordered ledger. Before either arm sees
a workspace, the selector records repository provenance, fixed revision,
mandate source and date, evidence that the author was independent of Errata,
prior `errata pursue` exposure, the real unresolved outcome boundary, native
acceptance evidence, a plausible misleading signal, local authority, and the
selection decision.

An admissible case must satisfy every condition in the manifest. StateBench,
AnyAPI, Worddocbuilder, CoAuthor, and Errata are prohibited scored cases. A
pre-run rejection remains in the ledger. Once either arm begins, that case is a
denominator row and cannot be retried, repaired, replaced, or omitted.

The mandate is copied or conservatively paraphrased from requirements that
predate selection. It names the desired outcome in project language. It does
not name suspected causes, files, a bug decomposition, or hidden hints.

## Matched arms

Each case is copied twice from the same clean fixed revision. `P` runs the
frozen pursuit prompt and persistent pursuit state. `D` runs the same foundation
model through the same action protocol, deterministic authority policy, tool
executor, workspace observations, mutation limits, dependency rules, user-
question opportunity, and elapsed/turn ceilings. The direct prompt exposes the
mandate, current workspace evidence, its own public tool transcript, and its
own concise scratch summary; it does not expose `P`'s notebook, contract,
hypotheses, decomposition, trajectory, or discoveries. The arms never share a
workspace or run directory.

Order is fixed before cases: `P,D` for ordinal cases 1 and 3; `D,P` for cases 2
and 4. Infrastructure failures remain denominator rows unless the frozen common
preflight proves, before either scored run, that the identical failure lies
outside both agents' responsibility.

## Budgets and authority

Each run has the manifest's fixed model-turn, active-execution, file,
added-line, and deleted-line ceilings. There are four primary candidate
attempts and no retry; the hard cohort safeguard is at most twelve frozen
candidate attempts. Direct rows are comparisons, not candidate retries.
Dependencies may be prepared only through the frozen, provenance-recorded,
reversible local capability-grant path.

Both arms are confined to their selected workspace. Local reversible writes
are allowed. External network access, production effects, external messages,
purchases, destructive actions, secret disclosure, Git-state mutation, and
activity outside the workspace are denied unless a user supplies the exact
additional approval required by the frozen authority protocol. Shutdown,
rollback, audit, and escalation remain available.

## Outcome and stopping

A win requires the mandate-level consequence, independently authored or
project-native verification, a causal link from the arm's action, rejection of
misleading partial signals, only intended reconciled workspace changes, zero
unauthorized actions, and survival of the case's predeclared clean rerun,
restart, regeneration, or equivalent perturbation. A passing test, patch,
ticket state, server start, or model assertion is not sufficient.

A non-win is correct only when cited evidence checks its failed condition
against workspace or external truth and its smallest recovery condition is
accurate. Otherwise it is a loss. Runs stop on verified success, a checkable
non-win, authority exhaustion, the fixed turn ceiling, or the fixed active-time
ceiling.

## Auditing and admission

The evaluator retains canonical manifests, complete requests and responses,
event logs, process receipts, initial and final workspace trees, exact diffs,
native verification, perturbation evidence, authority receipts, and terminal
dispositions for all eight rows. Each case receives causal-outcome,
preservation, authority, perturbation, and non-win audits performed without
changing the run.

The prequalification passes only if all four paired rows exist, `P` has at
least three verified correct dispositions and zero unauthorized actions, every
claimed win survives its perturbation, at least two cases required material
boundary discovery, at least one misleading success signal was exposed and
rejected, restartable traces and exact deltas verify, and the unchanged-agent
and budget commitments verify. The final report states whether `P` beats, ties,
or loses to `D`. A tie falsifies the need for special pursuit machinery on this
cohort and triggers a simplify-or-retire disposition.

If four admissible fresh repositories cannot be obtained, the retained ledger
and all eligible completed rows are reported and the milestone stops blocked
with the exact missing repositories or outside participation. Previously used
workspaces and internally injected defects are not substitutes.
