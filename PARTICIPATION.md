# Participation and data boundary

This is a research recruitment record, not a bounty, employment offer, or
promise of publication or authorship. No compensation is offered unless a
separate written agreement says otherwise. Credit is opt-in and records the
actual role; participation does not imply endorsement.

## Eligibility and role separation

A scored situation or capsule author must not have seen the applicable
candidate implementation, prompts, development cases, or traces. Someone with
prior exposure may still review public material but cannot author a scored
case. For one case, candidate developer, author, custodian, and outcome
adjudicator are distinct people; a synthetic author cannot custody their own
capsule. Affiliations, funding, prior collaboration, authoring tools, models,
prompts, data sources, and collaborators are declared before eligibility is
accepted.

The submitter must have authority to provide the material for this evaluation
and must disclose its license and restrictions. Do not submit employer or
client property, personal data, credentials, export-controlled material,
malware, production access, or material whose disclosure or execution would
violate law, contract, policy, or another person's rights.

## Withdrawal, retention, and publication

A volunteer may withdraw before their material is sealed into a public
pre-draw manifest. After sealing, the commitments, receipts, decisions, and
results are retained to prevent selective deletion; raw private material is
retained or destroyed only under the custodian's published policy. Embargoes
and publication limits must be accepted before transfer. A case is not run if
those terms are unresolved.

An accidental public disclosure invalidates that case. Deleting an issue or
comment cannot reliably recall notifications, caches, or history.

## Real-software model-provider boundary

The real-software candidate uses OpenAI's `gpt-5.6-sol` through Codex. A run may
send the outcome mandate, selected workspace path, the agent's notebook,
selected source or documentation snippets, and redacted tool output to OpenAI.
No private repository or submitted material enters a run until a person legally
entitled to authorize it gives written, case-scoped approval covering the
provider, exact allowed data categories, applicable OpenAI account terms and
retention setting, redaction, and logging. Secrets and personal data are not
accepted model context. Withholding that approval is a valid pre-run rejection,
not a failed agent attempt.

## Current intake status

Public issues accept role, eligibility, and contact declarations only. No
sealed material is currently accepted. An eligible outside custodian must first
publish their identity/key commitment, encrypted endpoint, receipt format,
retention/deletion policy, breach procedure, and chronology boundary. Candidate
developers must not control or access that channel before closure.
